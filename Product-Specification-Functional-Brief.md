Core PurposeTo provide a zero-friction, automated job-hunting platform that aggregates listings across multiple boards, organizes them into three targeted career and income streams, and equips each listing with pre-compiled custom application materials and automated submission triggers.2. Stream 1: Core IT & Systems EngineeringFocus: Mid-level enterprise IT infrastructure, cloud platforms, identity administration, and technical support.Target Domains: Microsoft 365, Microsoft Entra ID, Azure, Windows Server, PowerShell, containerization, Tier 2/3 systems administration, and DevOps.Objective: Secure the primary next career step with compensation and responsibility matching mid-level systems engineering experience.Stream 2: Local "Bridge" & Casual WorkFocus: Immediate, low-barrier local employment requiring no specialized certifications or prerequisites.Target Domains: Hands-on operational roles, retail, light warehouse, driver/courier, or local event/venue assistance within St Kilda, Balaclava, and inner Melbourne.Objective: Provide reliable casual or part-time income with scheduling flexibility while conducting the primary IT search.Stream 3: Technical Traineeships & Trade PathwaysFocus: Roles offering structured on-the-job training, apprenticeships, or employer-sponsored certifications.Target Domains:Telecommunications (structured cabling, fibre splicing, network rollout)Data centre operations (rack installation, power distribution, hardware maintenance)HVAC & mechanical services (commercial/residential installation, system maintenance)Objective: Expand career options into technical and hands-on trade disciplines with established learning pathways.3. Manual Route (Tailored Document Pack)Tailored Résumé (PDF / Markdown): Dynamically reorders bullet points and skills to emphasize the specific stack requested by the job description.Targeted Cover Letter: Addresses the company directly, highlighting relevant project milestones and technical background.Opening Email / Outreach: Generates a 3-paragraph outreach draft intended for direct contact with internal recruiters or hiring managers.Automated Route (One-Click Pipeline)Automated Action Button: Sends an authenticated webhook payload to the serverless proxy.Autofill / Submitter: Orchestrates the automated pipeline to stage candidate details, upload generated attachments, and execute supported application form fills.5. Filtering & Dashboard ControlsCategory Switcher: Rapidly toggle between Core IT, Local Casual/Bridge, and Trades/Traineeships.Work Style Filter: Isolate Onsite, Hybrid, or Remote roles across all three streams.Verification Filter: Separate verified employer routes from aggregate boards requiring manual link verification.Proximity Radius: Strict distance slider focused on the local area (Balaclava / St Kilda) for casual roles versus Greater Melbourne for IT roles.
