# Field Technician Track — Melbourne

**Candidate positioning:** Practical infrastructure technician with NBN Layer 1 deployment and fault-finding experience, enterprise hardware and endpoint lifecycle experience, Windows Autopilot and Intune deployment, data-centre-adjacent infrastructure knowledge, and strong diagnostic and documentation skills.

## Exclude or investigate carefully

- **Hitachi Energy Junior Field Service Technician:** Requires a qualified electrician.
- **CBRE electrical/HVAC shift roles:** Strong practical fit but likely require trade qualifications and rotating 12-hour shifts.
- **Porter Equipment, William Adams, and Kubota:** Excellent practical environments, but heavy-equipment or diesel qualifications may be mandatory.
- **Generic service-desk roles:** Don’t satisfy the goal of leaving the corporate desk environment.
