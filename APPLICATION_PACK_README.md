# Sam Ludwig — Application Pack

Prepared August 8, 2026.

## Download format

Résumé and cover-letter buttons in the dashboard open the employer-facing PDF files. Markdown links are kept as optional editable sources and aren’t the primary application files.

## How to use each role

1. Open the application route and confirm that it is still available.
2. Review the role-specific requirement checks in the tailored résumé, cover letter, and opening email.
3. Open the résumé PDF and cover-letter PDF from the dashboard.
4. Use the opening email only when the employer publishes a direct email or contact form, or when you choose to contact the employer through the listing.
5. Review the wording, attach the résumé and cover letter, and send it yourself.
6. Record the outcome in your own application tracker.

## Contact details

A hiring manager, phone number, or email appears only when the employer publishes one. When no direct contact is available, use the listing or employer contact form. The application route on each dashboard card identifies whether the link goes to a job board, employer site, government portal, council portal, or another verified route.

## Fit audits and review

Each role has a separate JSON fit audit. The audit records the screening classification, supported terms, unverified or missing terms, requirements to confirm, and a manual review checklist. It’s a prioritisation aid, not a guaranteed Applicant Tracking System score.

Use the audit to decide what to check. Don’t paste it into the résumé, cover letter, or email.

## Important checks

- LinkedIn is excluded.
- No applications or emails have been submitted.
- The documents don’t claim licences, trade certificates, horticulture qualifications, RSA, hospitality experience, electrical qualifications, vehicle access, security clearances, or other requirements that haven’t been confirmed.
- Some roles are deliberate career-change or local-work applications. Check the requirement notes before applying.
- GPK is an existing pipeline item with a short video response pending. Don’t submit a duplicate application without checking the existing process.
- Read the listing, audit, résumé, cover letter, and opening email before any manual submission.
